      Load reduction tips

      💧 Shift heavy loads

      Run the geyser, stove and washing machine outside peak windows (05:00–09:00 and 16:00–22:00).

      ⚡ Drop the geyser breaker

      A 3 000 W geyser is your biggest load. Switch it off at the breaker and only heat when needed.

      🌙 Go LED

      Replace halogen downlights with LEDs — up to 80% less lighting load per room.

      📶 Unplug standby

      Decoders, chargers and TVs on standby still draw 300–800 W across a house.

      🔋 Stagger kettle & microwave

      Never run kettle + microwave + toaster together — each is 1 500–2 500 W on the same circuit.

      ⚙️ Protect the fridge

      Keep the fridge/freezer closed — it stays cold for hours. Only essentials belong on your backup.
